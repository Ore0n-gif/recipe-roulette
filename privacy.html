Privacy policy

Recipe Roulette · last updated 2 November 2026

Short version: Recipe Roulette has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Your recipes stay on your phone.

What the app stores

Your own recipes, photos, favourites, week plan and settings are saved only on your device. The developer has no server and never receives them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, so use Settings → Save backup to keep a copy.

Your Claude API key (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, it is stored only on your device. When you tap New idea or Import, the app sends your request (for example the link, text or screenshots you chose to import) straight from your phone to Anthropic using that key. Anthropic’s own privacy policy applies to those requests: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Claude chat

If you choose Ask in Claude chat, the app opens claude.ai (or the Claude app) with the request filled in and copies it to your clipboard. Nothing is sent until you send it there yourself.

Sharing recipes

When you share a recipe, the recipe itself is packed into the link. It goes only to the people you send it to. Your own photos are never included.

Other connections

The app loads its font from Google Fonts, the web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, and photo credit links open Wikimedia Commons. These services may see your IP address like any website does. The app collects no analytics.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
